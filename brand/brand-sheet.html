ZIO Schema brand sheet, overview

The Z is a strand of DNA

In the ZIO Schema logo the Z of ZIO is one strand of a double helix. A violet backbone sweeps from top right to bottom left as the diagonal, and six base pairs hang from it: the top and bottom pairs are the bars, the four between them shorten toward the middle so the second strand is implied. Each pair joins a light half and a dark half, the way a field joins a name and a type. The tagline “The DNA of your data” is a proposal until it is confirmed.

1 Logo

ZIO Schema, light and dark

IO Schema
IO Schema

The wordmark is Space Grotesk 600 with −3% tracking, with the mark standing in for the Z at cap height on the baseline. The backbone's flat ends sit level with the top of the I and the baseline. The mark is never placed beside a full “ZIO Schema”, because that would say Z twice.

1b Symbol

The symbol: the Z on its own

The same Z used alone where there is no room for the name. On a 120-unit grid: a 10-unit backbone with a 14.5-unit Night outline, 10-unit bars, and 8-unit middle pairs, all six pairs at full strength. On dark every violet moves one step lighter and the outline becomes the page colour.

3 Type

Space Grotesk for words, JetBrains Mono for code

IO Schema
The DNA of your data
Schema[Person]

Wordmark and buttons in Space Grotesk 600, headings in 700, body in 400. Code, type names and small caps labels in JetBrains Mono. In code, keywords take Deep strand and type names take amber #9A5B13, the one warm colour, used only inside code. Logo files should convert the wordmark to outlines so no font is needed.

4 Clear space and size

Room around the logo

IO Schema

Clear space is one bar height (one twelfth of the mark, about a quarter of the cap height) on every side, shown by the dashed box.

Minimum size: the full mark from 48 px; the full logo down to a Z 16 px tall (23 px type). Smaller than that, use the simplified symbols in section 5.

5 Icons

Favicon, app icons and avatar

The icon is the dark-mode mark on a Night tile, so it shows on light and dark browser chrome alike. Three drawings, not one shrunk: full from 48 px, no middle pairs from 24 to 47 px, solid bars and backbone under 24 px. The circle version scales the mark to 68% for GitHub, Slack and Discord. Files: favicon.svg, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png, icon-512.png, avatar-500.png.

7 Usage

Do not

Don't draw the second strand. The Z turns into an X.
Don't rotate, skew or flip the mark. The Z only reads one way.
read the schema docs
Don't set text in Base light on white. It is 2.14:1; use Helix violet.
Don't add shadows, glows or gradients beyond the backbone's own.
