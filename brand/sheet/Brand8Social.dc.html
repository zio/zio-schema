Rules for every card
· One idea per card, in Display type, at most six words.
· Wordmark top left, or the mark alone when the copy already says “ZIO Schema”.
· The sweep sits behind, cropped by an edge, at 16% on light and 22% on dark.
· The two-tone pair runs along the base from the left edge, about a third of the width.
· Real code only, and short: three lines fit a square card at a readable size.
