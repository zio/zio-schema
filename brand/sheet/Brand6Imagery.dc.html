Brand sheet · 06 · Visual and imagery style

Base pairs, not test tubes

Every graphic is built from parts of the mark: two-tone pairs, the backbone's sweep, and the fading hourglass of rungs. No photos, no lab imagery, no glowing helixes. When we show data, we show real types and real code.
6.1
Graphic language
Pairs
A light half and a dark half. Use as bullets, dividers, progress and field lists. The split point moves; the two halves never swap colour within one list.
Types as values
The sweep
The backbone alone, large and cropped by an edge, in its own gradient. One per layout, behind headings on covers and hero areas.
Helix texture
Rows of pale pairs that shorten and lengthen, tracing the strand the mark leaves out. Background only, in violet 100 and 200, behind nothing that must be read.
6.2
Diagrams
A schema is drawn as a card of field pairs: the name on the light half, the type on the dark half. Arrows are thin Night lines with open heads; violet is kept for the thing being described.

6.5
Rules
Use
Pairs as bullets, dividers and field lists.
Use
Real types and real code in every example.
Use
The sweep, cropped by an edge, once per layout.
Avoid
Stock DNA renders, lab photos and glowing helixes.
Avoid
Drawing both strands: a full helix competes with the mark.
Avoid
Any gradient other than the backbone's own violet one.
