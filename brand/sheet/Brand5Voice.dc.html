Brand sheet · 05 · Voice and tone

Exact, plain and generous

ZIO Schema talks the way a good type signature reads: it says exactly what you get, in words a newcomer can follow, and it points out everything else you get for free.
5.1
Voice
Exact
Name the type, the codec and the version. Say what happens, not what it feels like. If a claim needs a footnote, it needs rewriting.
We are precise, specific, checkable.
We are not vague, hyped, hand-wavy.
Plain
Explain the shape before the theory. “A schema is a value that describes a type” comes before reification, and every term is defined the first time it appears.
We are clear, friendly, step by step.
We are not academic, clever, gatekeeping.
Generous
One description pays off many times, so say so: the codec, then the diff, the patch and the migration. Show the reader the next thing they get for free.
We are encouraging, practical, open-handed.
We are not salesy, smug, withholding.

5.5
Words
Use
schema · describe · derive · codec · record · enum · field · encode · decode · diff · patch · migrate
Avoid
magic · seamless · effortless · supercharge · blazing fast · revolutionary · simply · just
The DNA metaphor
Use it to introduce the idea: talks, landing pages, social cards. Keep it out of API docs and error messages, where the real names (Schema, Record, Enum) do the work.
