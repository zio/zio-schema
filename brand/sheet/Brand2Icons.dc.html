Brand sheet · 02 · Icon and favicon

Small, then smaller

The icon is the mark on a Night tile. An all-violet mark would vanish on a violet tile, so the tile stays dark and the mark uses its dark-mode violets. As the icon shrinks, detail steps down in three stages so the Z always survives.
2.1
App icon
Primary tile: Night #101521, corner radius 22% of the side, the dark-mode mark at 74% of the tile. Works on light and dark pages alike.
Alternate tile for dark surfaces that need a light object: white, a 1 px Line #D5DBE5 border, the light-mode mark at 74%.
2.2
Detail by size
Three drawings, not one drawing shrunk. 48 px and up: the full mark. 24 to 47 px: the middle pairs drop out and the bars and backbone get heavier. Under 24 px: solid bars and a solid backbone, cut apart by a gap in the background colour.

2.5
One-colour versions
For print, embroidery, stickers in one ink and monochrome badges. The base pairs keep a small gap between their halves so the pairing still shows without two tones, every pair is drawn in the same ink, and the backbone is cut free by a gap in the background colour. With no tints available at all (foil, embossing), use the 16 px drawing on the right.
