Three violets and a Night

ZIO Schema takes violet, the one hue no sibling uses: Config is orange-red, Prelude terracotta, Logging amber, HTTP green, Blocks blue and JSON raspberry. Three violets come straight from the mark, the backbone and the two halves of every base pair. Night and Paper carry everything else.

Every ZIO library owns one hue. Schema's violet (254°) sits 24° from Blocks' blue (230°), but is brighter and more saturated; it is 82° from JSON's raspberry and far from the warm three and HTTP's green. Never use a sibling's hue in Schema material, and never use violet in theirs.
