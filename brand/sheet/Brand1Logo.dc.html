A Z with a helix inside

Mark 56: one violet backbone sweeps from top right to bottom left as the Z's diagonal, and six base pairs hang from it. The top and bottom pairs are the bars; the four between them shorten toward the middle, so their ends trace the second strand without drawing it. Each pair has a light half and a dark half, the way a field has a name and a type.
1.1
Primary mark
Light mode. Backbone runs from Helix violet #6D4AE0 to Deep strand #4A2FA6, outlined in Night #101521. Base pairs in Base light #B7A6F7 and Deep strand; all six pairs at full strength.
Dark mode. Every violet moves one step lighter: backbone #9C86F2 to #8A72F0, pairs in #C9BCFA and #8A72F0, all six at full strength. The outline is the page colour, so the backbone floats free of the pairs.

1
Backbone
One strand of the helix, drawn as a flat-cut ribbon. It is the Z's diagonal, and it carries the gradient from light violet to deep.
2
Bars
The first and last base pairs, at full strength and full width. They make the top and bottom of the Z.
3
Middle pairs
Four pairs that shorten toward the middle, at the same strength as the bars. Their ends trace the hourglass of the second strand, which is never drawn.
4
Two halves
Every pair joins a light half and a dark half, like A with T. In a schema, a field is a name paired with a type.
